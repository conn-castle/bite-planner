Choose what to buy at $store on Instacart for this shopping list.

## Shopping list

$items

For each item, search for it and choose the product that best fits
what the meals need, and enough of it to cover the item's amount on
the shopping list. If two products fit equally well, choose the one
the family has bought before. Also give up to eight alternatives the
family could choose instead, or say why there are none. If $store
doesn't have an item, mark it not found, with alternatives to buy
and/or why the meal works without it.

When every item has a product or is marked not found, call the `done`
tool.

## Meals

These are the meals the shopping list is for. Use them to choose the
right products and alternatives, but buy only what's on the shopping
list, not these ingredients.
