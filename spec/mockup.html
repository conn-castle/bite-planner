Order cards, next step first

Home is a stack of order cards, each with its progress track. The top card says plainly what to do now, with one button. The other cards fold their meals into a stack; tap it to see the list.

Home state
The create-order flow is live from any state: tap the top card's button or +. Close it partway and the draft stays on Home to resume or discard. Suggestions take about 7 seconds here (1–2 minutes for real); Generate more takes about 6. Household settings move the draft's decide-by and reminder dates live.
