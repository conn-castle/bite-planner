# Interface specification

The accepted interface contract for Bite Planner. Functional behavior is defined in [requirements.md](requirements.md); this document records shared patterns and intentional differences from the original mockup.

- [`requirements.md`](requirements.md): the implemented functional contract and deferred scope.
- [`mockup.html`](mockup.html): the UI, as a clickable mockup with mock data. Open it in a browser; the panel on the left switches between home states. It is a historical visual reference, not reusable application code.

Where the two disagree, the requirements win. Decisions from reviews of the built app also win over the mockup:

- Every row of days is one shared component (`apps/mobile/src/ui/DateBar`), configured with props (e.g. the compact strip on home) but sharing the same parts.
- The delivery date’s day row starts at today, including when earlier orders still cover future meals, and reaches back to an expired saved choice. Today is selectable when a remaining live window is available.
- The full-screen ordering flow is Pick meals → Servings → Shopping list → Delivery → Next delivery → Review. Shopping starts after Servings; the final review authorizes booking.
- The next-delivery day row marks the days that have meals with a bar in the order's color, starts at the delivery day, and still marks the best day.
- Placed order cards (home and the order view) have a 1px border in the order's color at 40% (`color-mix(in srgb, var(--c) 40%, transparent)`), not the mockup's neutral hairline, so each card matches its bar in the day strip.
- The bar of an order not delivered yet is solid, in a lighter tint of the order's color (45% of it on white), not dashed as in the mockup.
- While meal ideas are being made, the draft card shows how long it has taken ("Working for 0:42") instead of the mockup's made-up estimate ("About 1 min left").
- The draft card doesn't credit picks to a person ("2 meals added", not "Sam has added 2 meals"): anyone in the household can pick, and the order records only who started it.
- A placed card's last track node (its days) shows no progress ring before the first day; the mockup's empty ring still draws its round line end as a dot.

- A meal's rating is one of four exclusive values, worst to best: "not for us" (the ✕ while picking), thumbs down, thumbs up, and favorite (a favorite is always liked, so it can't be thumbs down). No rating is the fifth state. Discarding a draft keeps its "not for us" marks.
- While picking, a suggestion has only the ✕. Thumbs and the heart are for meals of placed orders.

Ratings and history extend the original mockup:

- A meal's page has one round button at the end of its toolbar: "?" when not rated, otherwise the rating. Tapped, it grows sideways into the three choices (thumbs down, thumbs up, heart); tapping the chosen one clears it.
- The prompt to rate last week's meals is on the "Finding meals" step while suggestions generate: one card per order, in the look of History's order cards, titled "How were these meals?", with the three choices beside each meal.
- History has a search field and filter chips (All, Favorites, Liked, Didn't like, Not rated) under the "By order" / "All meals" switch, and each meal shows its rating as a small mark.

Settings, as reviewed:

- The mockup's Dislikes row isn't built, and won't be: ratings and "not for us" cover it.
- Members supports invitations, withdrawing invitations, and removing members. Anyone may leave, but the last member cannot be removed. Removal ends access, device notifications and reminders, and releases their order lock while preserving account attribution.
- Default servings is a stepper (1–12), like the decide-by and reminder days, not the mockup's placeholder row.
- The Household group starts with the household's Name; Account has Your name and Email. A name changes in a small dialog.
- Settings has no preview of the next order's dates (the mockup's "With these settings, Order N"): settings hold settings.
- The "By order" / "All meals" switch's track is a tint of ink (8%), not the mockup's `--bp-fill-strong`, which is all but the color of the warm page behind it.

Notifications, as decided:

- "Your meal ideas are ready" goes only to the member who started the batch.
- Tapping a notification opens the order flow only when the household's draft is the one it's about (a reminder's order, or the draft whose ideas are ready); otherwise it opens Home with an alert saying why (already placed, not started yet, or the draft was discarded), and Home's top card has the next step. A tap never starts a draft (the mockup opens the flow either way).
- In the app, iOS shows no banner; "ideas ready" shows as the mockup's toast unless the order flow is open. Reminders show nothing extra (Home already shows the order's state).
- Settings' Personal group has a "Daily reminders" switch (each member's own, kept by the server; on by default): off, that member gets no daily reminders. "Your meal ideas are ready" has no switch: it goes only to whoever asked for the ideas. While iOS doesn't allow Bite Planner's notifications on the phone, the group starts with a row saying so and a button: Allow (iOS's prompt, the first time) or Open Settings (after that). The mockup's "Allow notifications: On" row isn't built.
- Where the mockup says "yours at 8:00 AM", a member whose reminders are off (the switch, or iOS) sees "yours are off" (the draft card: "your reminders are off").
- The Next delivery step's card has no mini strip of days (the mockup's `.mini-strip`): the day row under it already marks the days the meals cover.
- The Next delivery step says what it sets for the following order in one line ("Order 5: decide by …, reminders start …"), not the mockup's "What this sets" group.

The shopping list, as built (stages 1 and 2; the mockup has the list step but not its waiting, its on-hand items, or changing amounts; in the flow, the shopped list below replaces its items, and a placed order from before the checkout implementation still shows them):

- While the list is made, its step shows the same waiting screen as "Finding meals" (the orb, how long it has taken, Notify me), titled "Making your shopping list". "Your shopping list is ready" goes to whoever left Servings, and its tap opens the flow at the list.
- Items assumed on hand are behind an info button under the list's summary ("ⓘ 3 items assumed on hand"); tapping it opens a small dialog in the middle of the screen (ui/InfoDialog) listing the ones these recipes use, closed with Got it.
- The list has a sixth aisle, Other (after Pantry), for items no other aisle fits; the mockup has five.
- Each aisle is a card with a thin border on the white page, its head a band in the aisle's own soft color with its name and count ("Produce … 4"); the mockup has a small grey heading with the count at the far right. The aisle colors are their own set (theme/tokens.css), apart from the order colors. It leaves room for a product picture at the start of each row later (stage 3).
- In the list step, each item ends with its amount's stepper (whole steps for things counted, ¼ or ½ for small amounts in units) and a red ×, not the mockup's amount at the start and − / Restore. The × is red with no background; tapping it crosses the whole item out (its name in red) and greys out its stepper at the amount it had, and tapping it again puts the item back. Once an amount was changed, the line under the item's name says "Recipes need 2¼ lb · Reset" (in place of its meals, so nothing moves), and Reset puts it back to that amount (the server keeps both). The controls stay at the row's end, where iOS puts a row's controls (Apple's guidance leads a row with its picture or text). A placed order's list (read-only) keeps the mockup's amount at the start.
- The list's summary is "to order", "removed", and the delivery day, as mocked.
- The Review step (not in the mockup) starts with the order as a card in its color ("Ready to place", when it arrives, the days it covers, the counts of meals, servings, and items, and the next order's dates, which aren't decided yet: "Order 5 would need to arrive by Fri 16 · decide by Thu 15"; no meal pictures, since the meals are listed under it), then its meals, dates, and shopping list as rows that go back to change them.
- A recipe opened from Pick meals, picked or not, has no servings stepper: its amounts are as written, its Ingredients heading has an asterisk, and a footnote under the list (ui/Note) says how many servings the recipe is for and that shopping prepares it for the servings chosen in the next step; servings are chosen only in the Servings step.

The shopping list shopped at Instacart (not represented in the original mockup), as reviewed in the design (2026-09-27) and replacing the list step's items above in the flow:

- A card's × (a pick's "don't show meals like this", a product's "don't buy this") is one component (ui/CornerToggle): red, on a white circle a little off the card's top-right corner; while it's off (a pick marked "not for us", a product not bought) it shows ↺ there instead, to put it back.
- A recipe's servings go back to what was chosen (an order's servings, else the recipe's own) with a reset icon to the left of the stepper, shown only once they've been changed.
- A pushed page (a recipe, a product) always shows its title in the toolbar, not only once its heading scrolls away.
- On wide screens (700px and up), the shopping list's product tiles are four across instead of two.
- Pick meals cards are one across below 680px, two from 680px, and four from 1320px. Three equal mini boxes sit in one row: ingredient icon and distinct non-staple ingredient count above Ingredients; clock and numeric minutes followed by min above Total; person icon and numeric minutes followed by min above Hands-on. The count includes seasonings; Total is elapsed time from starting to ready to eat, and Hands-on is active work. Missing times show a value-sized muted dash with the accessible name "Total time not listed" or "Hands-on time not listed", without a unit. Cards retain their meal artwork and Add/Added/not-for-us controls.
- Continuing an order opens the flow at the furthest step it has reached, by anyone in the household, not at picking meals (as mocked); the member can go back from there. The order keeps that step; when the order no longer supports it (say the picks changed since the list), the flow opens at the step that comes first, as it does mid-flow.
- The whole order flow is full-screen over the app at every screen size (not Ionic's centered dialog on wide screens), closed with its ×: it's a long task, and a stray swipe mustn't close it.
- The flow is Pick meals, Servings, Shopping list, Delivery, Next delivery, Review: the list comes right after Servings, so shopping starts as early as it can. The list step's waiting screen covers making the list ("Making your shopping list") and then shopping it ("Shopping at Wegmans"); "Your shopping list is ready" goes out once it's shopped ("12 products for Order 5. 1 item to decide on.").
- The list step shows the Wegmans products chosen for the list, by aisle, in the list's aisle cards; the list of things to buy isn't shown in the flow any more. Each product is a tile, two across: its picture on top, centered; the red × a little off the tile's top corner (tap it to not buy the product, again to buy it after all); the item from the list in bold ("Celery stalks"), and under it the product in small grey text (cut off with … after two lines), with an icon first when the household has bought it before (no legend); then its price at the bottom, left-aligned ("$8.07", with "$2.69 each" in small grey text when there's more than one, "est." for weighed items), and a stepper across the tile for how many. A product not bought stays in its aisle, its tile on the grey fill, the picture faded, the item crossed out in red, "Not buying" in place of the price, and the stepper greyed out. The summary is products, the total before fees, and how many to decide.
- Tapping a product opens its own page, pushed onto the flow like a recipe, with Back: its picture, the item in large bold type and the product under it (bought-before icon first); a group with Buy (a switch) and How many (a stepper, greyed out while Buy is off); while it's not bought, the picture is faded and the item crossed out in red, as on its tile, and nothing moves; Details (price, with "$2.69 each" or "estimate"; size; bought before, Yes or No; what the recipes need), with the meals it's for under them; and its alternatives (up to eight, each with Use, which switches to it and goes back to the list; the product it had becomes an alternative).
- The product page labels the curated choices "Recommended alternatives (#)". "More alternatives" is the final row inside that list, not a separate group above it; its saved results are excluded from the recommended count. The action pushes a separate page; Back returns to the recommendations, while Use returns to the shopping list with the chosen product (review decision, 2026-10-11).
- Items Wegmans doesn't have come first, in a red card like an aisle card ("Not at Wegmans"), each with Skip and Alternatives; Next stays off until each is skipped or has an alternative. Alternatives opens the item's page: "Curry leaves", "Wegmans doesn't have it", a Skip switch, what the recipes need, what it's for and why the meals may work without it, and its alternatives.
- A placed order's list shows its products' tiles, read-only: no × and "Qty 2" in place of the stepper.
- When the agent's work doesn't finish (meal ideas, the list, shopping), its waiting screen says so plainly and says what's kept ("We couldn't finish shopping at Wegmans this time. Your list is saved."), with Try again as a button of its own under the red notice; the error itself goes to the logs, not the screen. A list made but never shopped is shopped by itself on opening the step, like a list not yet made.

Delivery, booking, and tip (stage 3, updated 2026-10-18 by owner agreement; replaces the previous tomorrow minimum and later-arrival fallback):

- Delivery reads a checkout quote and all offered days, including Today, and shows the remaining recorded windows. While checking, it uses the same full waiting screen as final review, with elapsed time, Close/Notify me, and notification taps returning to Delivery. The screen says the check can take a few minutes; it never presents an all-disabled picker while loading. Successful and failed checks notify the requester when notifications are enabled. Choosing a window saves its ending time as the latest authorized arrival. Dates beyond the offered horizon remain available for planning and say availability will be checked later. Times use the delivery address’s time zone. An expired saved date/time stays visible and requires an explicit replacement.
- Final review reuses the delivery quote while products, counts, tip, address and availability are current. It shows the estimated total and account details using the existing single total row and Instacart group. The delivery row states that a later arrival needs approval. Delivery choices and their estimated total are reused for 12 hours, while products, counts, tip and address match. Passed windows are removed and the checked time is shown; Check again explicitly refreshes. Booking always checks live availability and offers alternatives requiring approval when the selected delivery is gone. Alternative proposals retain their 30-minute approval lifetime. An expired snapshot requires returning to Delivery and checking times; changing dates never silently selects another date.
- Approval must match the explicitly saved date and latest arrival time. Booking uses the latest actual window on that date ending by that deadline, never a later-ending window or another date. A date in the fresh offered snapshot books immediately; future planning retains the four-days-before, 9 AM scheduled booking. Before purchase, unavailability produces a live alternative with Approve delivery and Decide later. Approving authorizes that exact proposal; deciding later starts no purchase. A collision with the next delivery requires explicitly selecting a later next date. Existing change/cancel restrictions remain.
- Delivery addresses are searched in Settings as the user types, using AWS autocomplete, and the normalized address is shown for approval before saving. Shopping and checkout select exactly one matching saved Instacart address; missing or ambiguous matches fail. A changed destination invalidates draft shopping and totals; approved orders keep their approved destination. This supersedes the 2026-09-30 decision to use the account default.
- Instacart opens a new day's windows at midnight, five days ahead (measured 2026-09-29: at 12:08 AM Tuesday it offered today through Sunday), so a delivery on day D is first offered on D−5 and booked on D−4.
- "Waiting to book" shows on the order card's dot track: the Placed dot is the current one, not checked, until the order is booked.
- Booking never goes without an approved product. A product gone by then is swapped for the first available of its alternatives, or the booking asks, by a household setting ("If a product is gone: Use an alternative / Ask me", default Use an alternative); with no alternative left, it asks.
- The agent reads checkout through browser accessibility tools and reports referenced elements. Code fills the cart and validates the quoted details. Booking has a separate one-shot purchase tool gated by approval and checkout checks.
- Booking runs at 9:00 AM Eastern on the day it's due; an order approved after that books right away. The scheduled run repeats every hour from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM Eastern: each run starts only the orders that are due and still waiting, so a run that failed is made up an hour later with nobody involved, and its schedule is watched by the same alarms as the reminders'. An order still waiting two hours after its booking time shows as Not booked ("It didn't book on Mon, Oct 5 as planned."), with Try again, which books it through the API, not the schedule, so it works when the schedule is what's broken. Lateness sends no notification: a push needs our code to be running, and whenever it is, the hourly run books the order.
- Booking mode is an explicit operator setting: off, dry_run, or live. Only live mode may purchase; dry runs must be labeled honestly. Repository documentation does not assert which mode an installation currently uses.
- An approved order's chip (Home's card, its page, the screen after approving) says where it is with the store, short, with an icon: Waiting to book (an hourglass), Booking, Order placed, Pick a time (a clock), Pick a product, Not booked, Check Instacart. The order's page has an Instacart group: its status, when it books or what Instacart confirmed, and what can be done (change, cancel, try again, "It was placed" / "It wasn't placed", Open Instacart orders).
- Booking's notifications (booked, with any swaps; needs a new time; needs a product chosen; not booked; check Instacart) go to whoever approved the order, and a tap opens that order, not the draft (the tap rule for reminders and ideas above is about drafts).
- Settings (household) adds a Delivery group: Delivery time (default 6:00 PM), and Tip, a percentage of the items' total (default 10%), with Maximum tip (default $15), which the tip never goes over (both steppers); and an "If a product is gone" group (Use an alternative, the default, or Ask me: two rows, a checkmark on the chosen one).

The order lock, as decided:

- The lock belongs to one opening of the order flow on one phone, not just to the member. Opening the flow on another phone (or on the same phone after the app was closed) takes over the member's own lock at once; the phone that lost it shows "You're placing this order on another phone" with "Order here" (not in the mockup).
- While the locked screen is open, it turns into the draft by itself once the other member closes the flow or their app goes away for 2 minutes.
- Discarding the draft is refused while another member is ordering (the locked card has no Discard, as mocked).
- The locked card and screen show the app's steps, not the mockup's four, and the card says what they're doing at that step ("Sam is choosing the dates and servings"; with the one-thing-per-step flow, each step's own words).

Meal pictures:

- A meal's picture is drawn from its ingredients, not generated: the agent tags each meal with its dish (plate, bowl, skillet, or tacos) and each ingredient it pictures with one of 26 icons and one of 16 colors (`bite_planner_agent.meals`; ingredient tags define the picture). Salt, oil, spices, herbs, and other small amounts aren't pictured.
- The picture (ui/art `MealArt`): the main ingredient (the first fish, shrimp, drumstick, steak, pork chop, ground meat, tofu, egg, beans, or chickpeas; else the first side) large, up and to the right, in front; under it its bed (the first rice, pasta, sauce, or tortilla, drawn as its surface; else the next ingredient); two sides behind it, the first lying along the dish's lower-left edge. All in the dish seen from above (a plate with the ring of its well, a bowl, a skillet with its handle), or tacos side-on with the tortilla as the shell. A tint of the first ingredient fills the picture's slot. No herb garnish.
- Icons are flat, in the app's ink line on warm fills, with no depth. Asparagus and green beans share one icon (stalks); a steak is a T-bone's teardrop with a band of fat and a round bone; pasta is five elbow macaroni.
- Meals from before pictures, and the agent's waiting screens, show the plain plate (ui/art `PlateArt`).

Cooking:

- A step's timer rings with a phone notification, scheduled when it starts, so it rings with the phone locked or the app closed, with a sound bundled with the app (iOS rings silently without one), and in the app too. It needs the phone's notifications; when they're off, the timer doesn't start and says how to turn them on (timers must ring while the app is closed).
- One timer runs at a time; starting another replaces it. It runs until it rings or the cook taps it to stop it: closing the cooking view or tapping Done doesn't stop it (over the mockup, where going back drops it), nor does the app closing. Reopening a meal's cooking view goes back to the step it was left at (Done starts it over); after the app has closed, to its running timer's step.
- Each step says how long it waits, for its timer, and which of the recipe's ingredients it uses, for "You'll need" (amounts at the servings shown on the recipe); the agent gives both with the recipe. Meals from before have neither.
- The screen stays on while the cooking view is open, and sleeps as usual once it closes.
- The cooking view is full screen over the app, like the order flow, closed with its ×. Start cooking is along the bottom of a recipe opened from Orders or History (above the tab bar), not a suggestion being picked.

Recipe feedback and detail selection:

- The recipe opened from Pick has an icon-only +/✓ toggle in its toolbar, matching the round Back control. Its accessible label names the Add/Remove action and recipe; it toggles the same pick as the grid, at the household's checkout default. The recipe's base yield stays two. Feedback is available on unselected suggestions too.
- Feedback sends a freeform request, then shows “Changing your recipe · 0:42” using server timestamps and says the member can keep choosing or close the app. Durable job state resumes on return; no new ready push.
- Accept / Reject / Revise. Only Accept of the exact proposal applies the full replacement. Revise uses the accepted recipe and prefills the earlier request for editing; no Restore original, chaining field, or reusable saved library.
- Failed or stalled selected edits require an explicit choice: Keep current recipe, or Try again with a new attempt that blocks until decided (Nick: “Option 1: Require an explicit choice”). Named links to every selected blocker appear at Servings, List and Review.
- Servings uses picture-status variation **a quiet red caption beside the cooking time**. The compact 40px picture keeps B’s small hourglass/question corner badge; normal-case status in the existing danger red follows time in the metadata line above the full wrapping meal name. Picture, badge, metadata/status and name form one recipe-opening button; the servings stepper stays separate. No under-row status strip/pill/button or C-style enlarged/outlined picture. Unchanged rows have no status/badge. Honest labels remain Still changing / Ready to review / Change failed · decide. Only Servings uses full names and the centered 680px body/footer column; other meal-row contexts retain their layout.
- The selected unresolved count remains beside Next in Servings’ sticky footer (“1 meal needs review” / “N meals need review”), with totals left and no selected top alert. Running, ready and failed all count; unselected changes do not. List/Review retain grouped selected guard notices. Unpick removes that meal’s count/gate; reselect restores it until decided. These choices supersede the earlier separate row action and selected top-panel decisions.
- Draft recipe edits remain available after shopping. Accepted inputs alone determine freshness; proposals and unselected acceptance preserve shopping work. Warn before replacing existing shopping work at every build/re-shop entry point and before irreversible selected acceptance when work is affected. Picks and servings remain freely reversible until replacement; Cancel starts nothing and does not reopen automatically. Unselected proposals do not expire at shopping. This supersedes the earlier before-shopping-only decision. See requirements “Shopping freshness” for the dependency matrix.
- Review shows the full recipe against the accepted version: red struck removals, blue additions, unchanged context, facts, all ingredient lines, cooking instructions, timers and step ingredient assignments, and both pictures from the shared ingredient artwork. Missing facts remain missing in the accepted recipe until an approved proposal supplies them.

## Reminder timing

Member reminders follow the phone's time zone. A daylight-saving time skipped by the clock has no reminder that day; a repeated clock time sends once. Members without a known time zone receive no scheduled reminder.

## Prepared recipes

The existing shopping-list run confirms practical ingredient quantities and matching cooking step text per meal before combining list lines. At the ordered servings, recipe and cooking views use that build's saved recipe without further rounding. Other serving counts remain a labeled provisional preview from the base recipe; Reset restores the order recipe. Before a current build succeeds the preview remains provisional. Historical builds label scaled ingredients and original step amounts honestly. This supersedes the deterministic recipe scaling described in the historical build order above; no new review session or serving-control removal is implied.
